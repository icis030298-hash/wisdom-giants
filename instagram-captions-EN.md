# Notes on tone

- **First line is everything.** IG cuts off at ~125 characters — the hook has to survive alone.
- **Short lines, lots of breaks.** Dense paragraphs get scrolled past.
- **Emoji: 1–2 max.** More reads as spam to English-speaking audiences.
- **"Free" belongs high up.** It's the strongest thing you have and it's easy to miss.
- **Founder voice beats ad voice.** "I built this" gets far more engagement than "Introducing…"
